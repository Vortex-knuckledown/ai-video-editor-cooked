# motion-film (vendored, MIT)

From github.com/echris6/motion-video-kit (the `business-motion-film` skill's references), copied unchanged. See LICENSE.

- `references/quality-bar.md`: what a film must measure before it ships.
- `references/gauntlet.md` and `references/critic-prompts.md`: the review. A fresh critic sees only the render and
  the brief, pulls its own frames, and lists faults; the next round's new critic marks each FIXED, PARTLY or STILL PRESENT.
- `references/motion-grammar.md`: six rules and a catalogue of mechanisms.
- `references/product-hero-realism.md`: light and material for a hero object; section 5 lists rendering bugs to avoid.
