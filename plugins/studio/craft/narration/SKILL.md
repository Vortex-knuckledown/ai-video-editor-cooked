# narration (vendored, MIT)

From github.com/vincentsch/explainroo by Vincent Schmalbach: the rule sections of `docs/writing.md` (its first 58
lines) and `docs/pictures.md` (its first 30 lines), and `formats/vertical-short/guide.md`, copied unchanged. See LICENSE.

than a documentary needs: the story shape comes from long-video's `references/story.md`.
