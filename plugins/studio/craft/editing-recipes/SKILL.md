# editing-recipes (vendored, Apache-2.0)

From github.com/nexu-io/openmotion (`library/skills/openmotion-*`), each recipe copied unchanged. See LICENSE. They
are written recipes, not rendered templates: follow the numbers, build in this app's own way.

- `motion-graphic-placement.md`: one graphic every 5 to 8 s in a wide video, 3 to 5 s in a tall one, tighter in the
  first 15 s; it lands on its word, enters in 0.3 to 0.4 s, holds twice the reading time.
- `talking-head-recreation.md`: punch in to 110 to 120% on the key word, snapped in about 0.12 s.
- `multi-clips-to-reels.md`: cuts on the beat with a slow 6 to 8% push through each shot.
- `time-freeze.md`: a freeze frame with a flash and layers that move at different speeds.
- `caption-styling.md`: caption styles with their sizes, colours and timings.
- `shortlist-shorts.md`: which 20 to 75 seconds of a long video stand alone, scored before anything is cut.
- `multicam-edit.md`: when to cut between speakers.
