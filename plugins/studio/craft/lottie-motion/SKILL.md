# LottieFiles motion design tables (vendored, MIT)

Reference only. Em dashes were replaced; nothing else is changed.

- `timing-easing-tables.md`: durations and eases by what moves and how far.
- `choreography.md`, `multi-element.md`: stagger budgets, counter-motion, who leads and who follows.
- `disney-principles.md`: anticipation, follow-through, secondary action, squash and stretch, as numbers.
- `entrance-exit.md`: entries and exits (an exit takes 65 to 75% of the entry's time).
- `ambient-continuous.md`: ambient motion and its limits (10 to 20% of the main move), parallax ratios.
- `quality-checklist.md`: what to check before a scene is called done.
